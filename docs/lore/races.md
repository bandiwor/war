# The four races

The world of _War_ ended quietly, one district at a time. What the bodycam meets in the dark are
four things that moved into the space people left. Each race has an origin, a motive and a
weakness. The weakness is a rule of the simulation, so a player who learns the lore learns how
to survive. Every claim below is backed by a scenario test in `tests/integration/`.

## Zombies: the overgrown

**Origin.** A fungus out of the research complex's sealed wing. It takes root in the nervous
system, eats the eyes and ears first, and keeps the body walking to spread its spores. The
fungal caps glow faintly on shoulders and skulls; some of the dead still wear torn shirts and
work trousers.

**How they hunt.** By smell alone. They have no sight and no hearing. You lay a scent trail
(stronger when you bleed or run). It spreads and fades through a pheromone field that walls
block. They climb its gradient. One that has your scent leaves an alarm marker that pulls
others in, so a crowd gathers by itself without any leader.

**Weakness: light.** The fungus burns in light. They will not walk onto lit ground, and a
flashlight held on one makes it panic and flee toward the dark. A lamp is a wall to them, and a
burning flare lights the ground the same way until it gutters out.

**Sound.** Rasping, wet breath through a ruined throat; gurgles; feet dragging over grit; a
ragged shriek when they lunge. All noise, never a pitch.

## Robots: the custodians

**Origin.** Security units of an unknown make, still running a protocol nobody remembers
writing. Some are wheeled sentries, some are spider-legged stalkers. They have lens hoods and
red tally lights, and an antenna that keeps them in touch.

**How they hunt.** They see only through their cameras, so they see best in the light. They
share every sighting over their network. Several engaged at once split up to flank you. Hurt,
they back off into cover where your line of fire cannot reach. The first to spot you calls
reinforcements, and those arrive already knowing where you are. A sentry fires its laser when
it has a clear line.

**Weakness: their eyes.** A flashlight in the lens blinds the camera for seconds. A robot cannot
see behind itself, so you can approach from its blind side. A burning flare looks like a target
to it, and it will go to the flare instead of you.

**Sound.** Servo whine that glides with every move, clean identification beeps, the laser's
falling chirp. The radio chatter between units leaks into the bodycam as static and image
interference: the closer they are, the worse the feed.

## Plants: the patient

**Origin.** Something seeded in the forest and the overgrown walls. It is not hungry the way an
animal is; it is simply growing, and you are nitrogen. Bioluminescent vines grow from L-systems
(branching rules applied over and over) around their bases.

**How they hunt.** By reach and trigger. A wall flytrap is rooted: it bites what comes within
reach of its jaws and spits acid at whatever keeps its distance. The acid lands where you stood
and keeps burning. A creeper walks on root-legs. It leaps from a few metres, latches on and
drains you for as long as it clings.

**Weakness: they cannot follow.** A flytrap never moves; walk around its reach. A latched
creeper can be shaken off by struggling: turn hard, jump, push it away. The fall stuns it, and
that is the moment to shoot.

**Sound.** Dry rustles, stems creaking, the woody snap of jaws, a wet throat-pop and the hiss
of acid.

## Animals: the frightened

**Origin.** Not monsters at all. Wolves and boars came back into the empty districts, and the
night belongs to them now.

**How they behave.** By need, not script. Hunger grows; a fed animal rarely attacks first.
Wolves are pack animals. Each packmate nearby adds courage, while a lone wolf loses its nerve.
A packmate that spots you tells the others, even those behind a wall. A boar defends its
ground: it ignores you at a distance, warns you at the edge and charges inside it. Predators are
bolder at night. Being shot makes some angry and others run.

**Weakness: fear.** Wounds, gunfire, a flashlight in the eyes and lost packmates all take the
fight out of them. Kill one wolf and the others may scatter. Back away from a warning boar and
it lets you go.

**Sound.** Voiced, low and pulsed: growls, a snarl on the lunge, the boar's grunt. A hunting
wolf sometimes howls, and the howl carries far.
